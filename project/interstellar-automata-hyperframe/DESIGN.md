## Style Prompt
Interstellar Automata reads as a quiet astronomical instrument: concentric tick-marked rings rotating around a small luminous core, suspended in a deep starfield. The motion should feel like a precise mechanism slowly aligning itself, not like a kinetic sci-fi title sequence. Restrained, engineered, cinematic.

## Colors
- Deep void: #050608
- Cosmic mid: #0d1118
- Ring ink: rgba(240,240,240,0.78)
- Tick muted: rgba(240,240,240,0.32)
- Core glow: #ffe4b8
- Orbit accent: rgba(180,210,255,0.62)

## Typography
- Lora, Georgia, serif for the project label (matches the rest of the portfolio).
- Small italic caption for the subtitle, low contrast.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use neon hues or saturated sci-fi blues.
- Do not animate flashy lens flares, lightning, or particle bursts.
- Do not add UI chrome (HUDs, brackets, screen frames).
- Do not let the central mechanism stop — the rotation should feel perpetual.
