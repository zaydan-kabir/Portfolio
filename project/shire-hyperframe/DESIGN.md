## Style Prompt
A wartime recruitment poster reframed for a service that helps veterans access disability benefits. Aged cream paper, heavy slab-serif navy wordmark "SHIRE" anchored by a five-point star, brick-red service-stripe chevrons flanking the type, and a diagonal banner ribbon reading "Currently Building" across the upper third. The mood is honoring rather than nostalgic — restrained, warm, archival, with halftone grain on every surface.

## Colors
- Aged cream: #ece1c4
- Deep navy ink: #1a2c5b
- Brick red: #b62a1f
- Gold star: #c89432
- Soft shadow: rgba(26,44,91,0.18)

## Typography
- Alfa Slab One for the SHIRE wordmark — heavy, condensed, poster-weight slab.
- Anton for the diagonal banner ("Currently Building") — tall, narrow, all caps.
- Special Elite / VT323 for the typewriter-style tagline and discipline tags.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use modern flat colors or neon — stay in the cream / navy / brick / gold palette.
- Do not use any military insignia, ranks, branch logos, or recognizable national flags.
- Do not animate flashy explosions, particle bursts, or scanlines.
- Do not let the composition feel digital — every surface needs the halftone grain pass.
