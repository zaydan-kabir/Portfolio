## Style Prompt
A quiet wartime-poster homage for Shire — a service that helps veterans access disability benefits. Aged cream paper, a single hand-drawn-feel service medal hanging on a striped ribbon as the only illustrated element, an elegant high-contrast serif "SHIRE" wordmark, and restrained typewriter eyebrow / tagline copy. The whole composition should feel like a found archival print: confident, generous whitespace, halftone grain on every surface. Closer to "If the Cap Fits You" than to a busy recruitment poster.

## Colors
- Aged cream: #ece1c4
- Deep navy ink: #1a2c5b
- Brick red: #b62a1f
- Brass medal: #c89432
- Soft shadow: rgba(26,44,91,0.18)

## Typography
- Playfair Display Black for the SHIRE wordmark — high-contrast didone, elegant.
- Special Elite for the typewriter eyebrow, tagline, and credit copy.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not add chevrons, corner marks, frame rules, or stacked star ornaments.
- Do not use more than two type families at once.
- Do not let the composition feel digital — every surface needs the halftone + grain pass.
- Do not center every line. Asymmetric, generous spacing wins.
- Do not use military insignia, ranks, branch logos, or recognizable national flags.
