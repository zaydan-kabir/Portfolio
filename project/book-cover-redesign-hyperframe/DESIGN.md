## Style Prompt
A sharp editorial book-cover redesign preview: classical sculpture treated like a scanned plate, brutal hot-pink type, black negative space, and a sleek graphite field. The animation should feel like a cover being assembled on a light table: image exposure, type lockup, registration marks, and small print details. Clean, graphic, and bookish rather than decorative.

## Colors
- Hot pink: #ff00b8
- Black ink: #050505
- Graphite field: #111318
- Marble white: #f2eee6
- Muted shadow: rgba(5,5,5,0.42)

## Typography
- Archivo Black for large anonymous cover-study typography.
- Libre Baskerville for small literary cover details and edition notes.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not make it look like a generic product mockup.
- Do not use soft pastel gradients.
- Do not let the sculpture overwhelm the typography.
- Do not add floating UI cards inside the HyperFrame.
- Do not animate with twitchy or random motion.
