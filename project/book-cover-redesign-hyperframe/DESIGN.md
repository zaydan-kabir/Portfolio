## Style Prompt
A still-life of a paperback book standing on a dark editorial surface — cream cover face, three lines of heavy black display type ("BOOK / COVER / REDESIGN") locked centrally, a thin foil-red rule with a small editorial credit beneath, and a small red "Coming Soon" stamp tilted in the corner. The motion should feel like a cover proof being laid onto a desk: the book rises into frame, lines of type drop into place one by one, the stamp slams down. Quiet, bookish, confident — not flashy.

## Colors
- Editorial ink: #0a0d12 (background)
- Paperback cream: #ece6d8 (cover face)
- Display ink: #0a0a0a (cover type)
- Foil red: #c1281d (accent rule + stamp)
- Soft shadow: rgba(5,5,5,0.5)

## Typography
- Archivo Black for the stacked "BOOK / COVER / REDESIGN" display type.
- Libre Baskerville (italic + caps) for the credit line and editorial caption.
- Special Elite for the small "Coming Soon" stamp.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not bring back the classical sculpture imagery or hot pink wash.
- Do not stack more than three display lines on the cover face.
- Do not add floating UI cards, registration crosses, or extra metadata blocks.
- Do not animate twitchy or random motion — every entrance lands deliberately.
