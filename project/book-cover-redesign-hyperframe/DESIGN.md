## Style Prompt
A book-cover redesign in the spirit of Ottessa Moshfegh's "My Year of Rest and Relaxation" cover — but stripped of its specific title and author. A dark editorial canvas, a single classical reclining figure rendered in cross-hatched engraving, and three lines of brutal hot-pink display type stacked at the top reading BOOK / COVER / REDESIGN. The motion should feel like the cover being assembled on a black light table: the figure exposes from the shadow, the type drops in lockup, a small pink mark lands at the foot. Quiet, sculptural, and graphic.

## Colors
- Editorial black: #050505
- Soft graphite: #14161b
- Hot pink: #ff00b8
- Marble white: #f2eee6
- Muted shadow: rgba(5,5,5,0.5)

## Typography
- Archivo Black for the stacked BOOK / COVER / REDESIGN display type.
- Libre Baskerville (italic) for the small editorial mark at the foot of the cover.

## What NOT to Do
- Do not use any author or book title text — the redesign is anonymous.
- Do not stack more than three display lines on the cover face.
- Do not introduce additional hues beyond hot pink + black + cream.
- Do not crowd the figure with crop marks, plate codes, or floating UI.
- Do not animate twitchy or random motion — every entrance lands deliberately.
