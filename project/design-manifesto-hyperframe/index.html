Design Manifesto

During my years at Stanford, I often returned to Sylvia Plath’s fig tree.

Distance has a way of clarifying what familiarity obscures.

Design emerged as a mode of attention: a way of looking closely enough.

Creating rather than merely consuming; shaping experiences rather than passing through them.

meaning in the everyday

Politics a vocabulary for systems, power, and public life.

Philosophy a discipline for asking what a life is for.

Art a method for returning beauty to ordinary experience.
