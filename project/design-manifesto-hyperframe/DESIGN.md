## Style Prompt

An editorial manuscript study for Zaydan Kabir Amanullah's portfolio: pale paper, black ink, quiet typographic hierarchy, thin research lines, and restrained fig-tree references. It should feel like a living page from a design notebook, not a decorative poster. Motion is precise, literary, and calm: lines write in, rules draw, fragments settle, and small marks pulse as points of attention.

## Colors

- `#f0f0f0` paper background
- `#0a0a0a` primary ink
- `#8a8a8a` marginal gray
- `#d97957` warm annotation accent
- `#52666a` muted system-blue gray

## Typography

- `Lora` for reflective manifesto prose
- `VT323` for portfolio metadata, labels, and small technical notations

## Motion Rules

- Use short editorial reveals and stroke draws.
- Keep easing soft but controlled: `power3.out`, `sine.inOut`, `expo.out`.
- Loops may replay the complete composition in-browser, but individual tweens must use finite repeats.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use neon gradients, chrome effects, or marketing-card styling.
- Do not crowd the page with too much body copy.
- Do not use default blue, default gray, or generic sans-serif UI colors.
- Do not make the composition feel like a PDF preview; it should be designed as an animated work tile.
